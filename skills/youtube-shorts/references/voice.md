# Voice

Write like a person explaining something to a friend, out loud.

- First person, "I". Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Concrete beats abstract: a number, a tool name, a file, a minute count.
- Plain verbs. "Use", not "leverage". "Build", not "craft". "Help", not "empower".
- No em dashes; use a full stop or a comma.
- No dramatic fragments ("Game changer." "Not even close.").
- Cut throat-clearing: "So basically", "Let's dive in", "In today's video", "It's important to note".
- Cut hype words: unlock, supercharge, seamless, revolutionary, insane, next level, secret.
- No fake suspense ("And the answer will shock you").
- If a line sounds like an ad, rewrite it as what you'd actually say.

If your channel has its own voice file, read that instead; it wins over this one.
